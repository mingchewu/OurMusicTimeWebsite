01About關於

A bilingual path into musicianship 以雙語開啟音樂素養之路

Our Music Time is a bilingual music curriculum grounded in the Kodály Method. It pairs practical lesson plans with simple, singable song structures to awaken students’ musicianship and curiosity — ideal for group classes, general music and individual instrumental lessons, especially piano.

By teaching songs at the keyboard, the curriculum helps children understand and analyse the music they play, building a firm foundation for future study while strengthening sight-singing and aural skills — all in a relaxed, joyful atmosphere.

「我們的音樂時光」Our Music Time 是一個以高大宜教學法（Kodály Method）為基礎的雙語音樂教學課程。課程設計融合了實用的課案與簡單易懂的歌謠結構，旨在啟發學生的音樂素養與興趣，非常適合用於團體班、通識音樂課程以及個別樂器（特別是鋼琴）教學。

通過在鍵盤上進行歌曲教唱，課程不僅幫助孩子們建立對樂曲的理解與分析能力，也為他們未來的音樂學習奠定堅實的基礎，同時提升試唱和聽音能力，讓學生在輕鬆愉快的氛圍中，逐步養成良好的音樂感知與表達能力。

02Founder & Author創辦人與作者

Chiunglien Liu 劉瓊蓮

Our Music Time was founded in 2025 by Chiunglien Liu in San Jose, California. She is its CEO and the author of numerous books.

In the 2000s, Chiunglien combined Eastern and Western cultures with decades of teaching experience and pedagogical research, creating a solid foundation for students to learn music — particularly musicianship. Her work grew into sharing engaging, challenging activities with music educators. By stimulating both hemispheres of the brain, her methods encourage physical engagement with music, fostering a holistic environment where creativity grows alongside technical skill.

我們的音樂時光 OUR MUSIC TIME 於 2025 年由劉瓊蓮在加州聖荷西創立。她是我們的音樂時光的執行長，也是多本著作的作者。

2000 年代，瓊蓮結合東西方文化與數十年的教學經驗及教學法研究，為學生學習音樂，特別是音樂素養，奠定了堅實的基礎。瓊蓮的工作逐漸發展為與音樂教育者分享有趣且具挑戰性的教學活動。她的方法不僅激勵了教師，也啟發學生全心投入樂器學習。透過同時激發大腦左右半球，瓊蓮的方法鼓勵學生以身體參與音樂，營造強調創造力與技術能力並重的全人學習環境。

Read her full biography閱讀完整簡介

Chiunglien Liu earned her Master’s degree in Music Education from the University of Cambridge, where she was awarded the Cambridge Overseas Scholar Scholarship. Before Cambridge, she was a university lecturer in Taiwan, conductor of national and high-school student bands, director of a flute trio inspired by The Magic Flute, and an active flutist. She holds a Master’s in Music Education with a Kodály emphasis from the Conservatory of Music at Capital University, and a Master’s in Music Performance from the City University of New York, where she studied with flutist Bernard Goldberg.

She has performed with the Royal Chamber Orchestra and the Cambridge University Philharmonia, is a member of the Cambridge Philharmonic Society, and has appeared at King’s College Chapel, Ely Cathedral and the Rudolfinum in Prague. In California she is an active member of the Fortnightly Music Club, a flutist, and a judge for music competitions across the San Francisco Bay Area.

Her independently published works include five felt storybooks — Cobbler, Cobbler; Witch, Witch; Lucy Locket Lost Her Pocket; Charlie Over the Ocean; I’ve Lost the Closet Key — as well as The Bear Hunt, Our Folk Songs & Rhymes for Children, and Our Fabulous Music Plans for Private & Group Lessons, Volumes I–III.

劉瓊蓮於英國劍橋大學取得音樂教育碩士學位，並獲得劍橋海外學者獎學金。在前往劍橋之前，她曾任台灣大學講師、全國及高中學生管樂團指揮、長笛三重奏（靈感來自《魔笛》）指導老師，並活躍於台灣長笛演奏界。她於美國首都大學音樂學院取得以高大宜教學法為主的音樂教育碩士學位，並於紐約市立大學取得音樂演奏碩士學位，師承著名長笛家伯納德·戈德堡（Bernard Goldberg）。

瓊蓮擁有豐富的演出經驗，曾與皇家室內樂團及劍橋大學愛樂樂團合作。她是劍橋愛樂協會成員，並曾於國王學院教堂、劍橋伊利大教堂及布拉格魯道夫音樂廳等著名場地演出。移居美國加州後，目前為 Fortnightly Music Club 活躍成員、長笛演奏家及舊金山灣區音樂比賽評審。

她獨立出版多本教學教材與書籍，包括五本不織布書：《鞋匠》、《巫婆》、《露西丟了錢包》、《查理越過海洋》、《我的衣櫃鑰匙》，以及《抓熊記》和教師用音樂書：《給寶貝的音樂歌謠》、《給老師的音樂課程教案》第一、二、三冊。

Supplementary Teaching Materials輔助教材

Pitch and rhythm are abstract ideas. Song magnets make beat and rhythm concrete and playful, while lyric posters and picture cards carry that clarity onto the staff — for private and group lessons alike.

音高和節奏都是抽象又難懂的概念，透過「歌曲磁鐵」讓拍子和節奏的概念更有趣也更具體；再一併使用「歌詞海報」和「彩色圖卡」，即使在學五線譜上也能輕鬆學習音高和節奏。所有教材都非常適合用於個別和團體班的課程。

The three Ps of Kodály高大宜的三個 P

  1. PrepareSing, move and listen until the idea lives in the body.準備 — 透過歌唱、律動與聆聽，讓概念先在身體裡扎根。
  2. PresentName it, see it, and bring it to the keyboard.呈現 — 為它命名、看見它，並把它帶到鍵盤上。
  3. PracticePlay, create and return to it until it becomes yours.練習 — 反覆彈奏與創作，直到它真正成為自己的。

06Teacher Training師資培訓

Grow with us as a teacher 與我們一起成長為更好的老師

This course is designed not only for teachers starting with beginners or young students, but for anyone whose current students need specific pedagogy to overcome difficulties. Our online Elementary, Intermediate and Advanced courses cover 100 folk-song lessons, musicianship training, game and activity learning, and certification for the Our Music Time database.

本教師培訓課程不僅適合教授初學者，更將教導老師們如何運用獨特的教學法，有效克服現有學生在音樂學習中遇到的各種困難，無論學生的年齡或程度如何。線上課程涵蓋「初級、中級和高級課程」的完整教學體系，內容包含 100 首經典民謠的教學方法、系統性的試唱聽音和視譜培訓、豐富有趣的遊戲和活動教學，完成課程並通過考核後可獲得師資培訓證書資格。
